Counted the way the desk counts, as exported: each report once, by its id (the export’s pages overlap, so some reports come twice), in the row its tenant files it under. “Intake tagged” is what intake guessed from the reporter’s browser. It is not the cause, and it puts reports in the wrong group.

parser: a month-first parser, day-first tenants

The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so a booking for 7 April lands on 4 July. These reports come through the app, by email, and through the booking-v2 form, whose hint already asks for the day first. Fix: parse in the tenant’s locale.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type the month first

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, then reads the field the tenant’s way, day first. People type what the hint tells them, and the date flips. Only reports through the form, only from booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not reach this form: the hint and the way the field is read must agree.

one-day move: the date shifts by one day

Not a swap: day and month stay in place and the date moves by one. Tuesday shows as Monday, an invite arrives before the booking was made, a client in Lisbon gets Tuesday and one in Toronto gets Monday for the same meeting, and one tenant sees it “only in June”. That is a time-zone or daylight-saving offset, not a date format. The desk marked these reports in its notes and still filed them under parser by tenant. The parser fix leaves them as they are.

For the desk

  • A wrong date is a symptom, not a cause. Check whether day and month traded places before you merge a report into a date ticket.
  • Count by report id, never by export row.
  • Do not group by the intake tag. Group by the tenant: its locale and its booking form.
